## Mohammed Shami

Country: India

## Career Summary

Mohammed Shami is a stalwart of Indian fast bowling, known for his relentless seam position and lethal accuracy. Making his international debut in 2013 against Pakistan in an ODI, he quickly established himself as a premier wicket-taker across all formats. Shami became the fastest Indian bowler to reach 100 ODI wickets. In Test cricket, he has been a vital weapon in India's pace attack, especially during historic overseas series victories. He has also recorded a World Cup hat-trick and holds the record for the most wickets by an Indian in a single ICC Men's Cricket World Cup tournament.

## Major achievements

- Fastest Indian to 100 ODI wickets.
- Hat-trick in the 2019 ICC Cricket World Cup against Afghanistan.
- Leading wicket-taker of the 2023 ICC Men's Cricket World Cup with 24 wickets.
- Multiple five-wicket hauls in Test cricket across SENA countries.
- Purple Cap contender and consistent performer in the Indian Premier League.

## Playing Style

Shami is an exponent of conventional swing bowling with a perfectly upright seam that allows him to extract maximum movement off the pitch. His ability to hit the seam consistently makes him dangerous even on unresponsive tracks. He relies on a high-arm action, nagging length, and subtle variations in pace to outsmart batters. Shami is particularly lethal with the older ball, utilizing reverse swing effectively.

## Interesting Facts

- Shami was raised in a small village named Sahaspur in Uttar Pradesh and traveled long distances for training before moving to Kolkata.
- He is renowned for his friendly demeanor off the field, often sharing his culinary skills by posting cooking videos on social media.
- Overcame multiple severe injuries and personal setbacks to make successful comebacks at the international level.
