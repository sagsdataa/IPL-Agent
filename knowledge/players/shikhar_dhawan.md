## Shikhar Dhawan

## Basic information

Full Name: Shikhar Dhawan
Country: India
Role: Opening Batsman
Batting Style: Left-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm off break
IPL Teams: Delhi Capitals, Mumbai Indians, Deccan Chargers, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Punjab Kings

## Career Summary

Shikhar Dhawan is one of India's most successful modern-day opening batsmen, renowned for his aggressive strokeplay at the top of the order in all three formats of international cricket. Making his Test debut against Australia in Mohali in 2013, he scored the fastest century by a Test debutant, finishing with 187 runs. He was a vital pillar of the Indian limited-overs team alongside Rohit Sharma, forming one of the most prolific opening pairs in cricket history. Dhawan played a crucial role in India's victory at the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy, finishing as the tournament's leading run-scorer and winning the Golden Bat. He accumulated over 10,000 international runs across Tests, One Day Internationals, and Twenty20 Internationals before announcing his retirement from international and domestic cricket in August 2024.

## Major achievements

- Fastest Test century on debut (85 balls against Australia, 2013).
- Golden Bat winner at the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.
- Most runs in a single calendar year in the Indian Premier League (historically consistent run-scorer).
- First player in IPL history to score two consecutive centuries.
- Over 6,000 runs in the Indian Premier League, making him one of the tournament's all-time leading run-scorers.

## Playing Style

Dhawan is a dominant left-handed opening batsman known for his signature stance and fierce cut and pull shots against fast bowling. He excels on bouncy pitches by utilizing his back-foot play effectively. His trademark thigh-five celebration became iconic among cricket fans worldwide. While primarily aggressive in the powerplay, he developed the ability to anchor innings in longer limited-overs matches.

## Interesting Facts

- Earned the affectionate nickname "Gabbar" in domestic and international cricket.
- Holds the record for the most fours hit in the history of the Indian Premier League.
- Scored a century in his 100th ODI match, becoming the ninth player in history to achieve this milestone.
