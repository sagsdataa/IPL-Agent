## AB de Villiers

## Basic information

Full Name: Abraham Benjamin de Villiers
Country: South Africa
Role: Wicketkeeper-Batsman
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm medium
IPL Teams: Delhi Daredevils, Royal Challengers Bangalore

## Career Summary

AB de Villiers was a modern-day batting legend and one of the most destructive and innovative stroke-makers in international cricket history. Representing South Africa across 114 Test matches, 228 One Day Internationals, and 78 Twenty20 Internationals, he accumulated over 20,000 international runs. Nicknamed "Mr. 360" for his unique ability to hit the ball to any part of the field, he retired from all forms of cricket in November 2021, leaving behind a legacy as one of the game's ultimate entertainers and match-winners.

## Major achievements

De Villiers holds the record for the fastest ODI century, reaching the milestone in just 31 balls against the West Indies in Johannesburg in 2015, during an innings where he also scored the fastest ODI fifty (16 balls) and hit 16 sixes. He was named ICC ODI Cricketer of the Year three times (2010, 2014, and 2015). He captained South Africa in all three formats of the game and served as a pillar of the Royal Challengers Bangalore franchise in the Indian Premier League, where he scored over 5,000 runs.

## Playing Style

A supremely versatile and athletic player, de Villiers combined orthodox textbook technique with unorthodox 360-degree shot-making. Capable of batting anywhere in the order, he excelled both as an aggressive finisher in limited-overs formats and as a resolute anchor in Test cricket. His quick hand-eye coordination allowed him to reverse sweep, scoop, and ramp fast bowlers with ease. Beyond his batting, he was a world-class fielder and an exceptional wicketkeeper.

## Interesting Facts

Outside of cricket, de Villiers was an exceptionally gifted all-around sportsman in his youth, holding national records in swimming and athletics, and playing rugby, tennis, and golf at a high level. He was also an accomplished singer, releasing an Afrikaans pop album with singer Ampie du Preez in 2010.
