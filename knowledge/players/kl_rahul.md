## KL Rahul

## Basic information

Full Name: Kannur Lokesh Rahul
Country: India
Role: Wicket-keeper Batter
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm medium
IPL Teams: Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Punjab Kings, Lucknow Super Giants

## Career Summary

KL Rahul made his international debut for India in Test cricket against Australia in Melbourne in 2014. He quickly established himself as a versatile top-order batter capable of opening the innings or anchoring the middle order across all three formats. Known for his elegant stroke play and calm demeanor, Rahul became the first Indian batsman to score a century on ODI debut. He has captained India in limited-overs formats and has been a prolific run-scorer in the Indian Premier League, leading both Punjab Kings and Lucknow Super Giants.

## Major achievements

- First Indian cricketer to score a century on ODI debut.
- One of the few Indian batters to score centuries in all three formats of international cricket (Test, ODI, T20I).
- Held the record for the fastest fifty in IPL history (achieved in 14 balls during the 2018 season).
- Orange Cap winner in the IPL for scoring the most runs in a single season.
- Scored multiple Test centuries outside of Asia, including memorable hundreds in Australia, England, and South Africa.

## Playing Style

Rahul is widely regarded as one of the most aesthetically pleasing and technically sound batters in modern cricket. Primarily an accumulator who values proper cricketing technique, he possesses a wide range of shots all around the ground. His ability to switch gears effectively makes him lethal in both red-ball cricket and aggressive T20 formats. Behind the stumps, he is a reliable wicket-keeper with sharp reflexes.

## Interesting Facts

- He shares the record for the highest individual score by an Indian in T20I run-chases.
- Rahul is named after the legendary cricketer Sunil Gavaskar's son (Rohan), though his parents mistakenly believed Gavaskar's first name was Rahul.
- He holds a degree in Bachelor of Commerce.
