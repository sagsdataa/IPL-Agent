## Steve Smith

## Basic information

Full Name: Steven Peter Devereux Smith
Country: Australia
Role: Top-order Batsman
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Leg-break
IPL Teams: Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Pune Warriors India, Rajasthan Royals, Delhi Capitals

## Career Summary

Steve Smith began his international career in 2010 primarily as a leg-spinner and lower-order batsman before establishing himself as one of the premier Test batsmen of his generation. He rose to the number one spot in the ICC Test batting rankings through consistent run-scoring across all major cricket-playing nations. Smith served as the captain of the Australian men's cricket team, leading them through notable series victories, including the 2017-18 Ashes. Despite a significant career interruption due to a suspension in 2018, he made a successful return to international cricket, playing a crucial role in Australia's 2019 Ashes retention in England and their victory in the 2023 ICC World Test Championship.

## Major achievements

- Won the ICC Test Player of the Year award in 2015 and 2017.
- Awarded the prestigious Allan Border Medal multiple times as Australia's best male cricketer.
- Scored centuries in both innings of a Test match multiple times.
- Integral member of Australia's 2015 ICC Cricket World Cup winning team.
- Winner of the 2023 ICC World Test Championship with Australia.

## Playing Style

Smith is renowned for his highly unorthodox batting technique, characterized by extreme shuffle movements across the crease, a pronounced backlift, and a grip that utilizes the bottom hand heavily. Despite his unconventional aesthetic, he possesses exceptional hand-eye coordination, extraordinary concentration, and an insatiable appetite for big scores. His unique stance allows him to manipulate the field effectively, and he excels at reading the length of the ball early, making him exceptionally difficult to dismiss in traditional Test match conditions.

## Interesting Facts

- Smith initially played international cricket primarily as a spin-bowling all-rounder before completely transforming his game to focus on his batting.
- He holds the record as the second-fastest batsman to reach 25 Test centuries, behind only Sir Donald Bradman.
- Beyond cricket, Smith played grade cricket in England and briefly pursued a professional career in grade rugby before fully committing to cricket.
