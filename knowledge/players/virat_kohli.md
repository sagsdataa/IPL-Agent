## Virat Kohli

## Career Summary

Virat Kohli is one of the most prolific and successful batsmen in modern cricket history. Making his international debut for India in August 2008 against Sri Lanka, he quickly established himself as a linchpin of the batting lineup. He has represented India across all three formats, leading the national team as captain in Tests, One Day Internationals, and Twenty20 Internationals with immense success. Known for his supreme consistency, exceptional run-chasing ability, and elite fitness standards, Kohli has rewritten record books. He holds the record for the most centuries in One Day Internationals, surpassing Sachin Tendulkar's long-standing milestone, and has amassed tens of thousands of international runs, anchoring India's batting order for over a decade and winning major ICC tournaments along the way.

## Major achievements

* Fastest batsman to reach 8,000, 9,000, 10,000, 11,000, and 12,000 runs in One Day Internationals.
* Most centuries in One Day International cricket history.
* Player of the Tournament in the ICC Men's T20 World Cup in 2014 and 2016.
* Leading run-scorer in the history of the Indian Premier League.
* Recipient of the Sir Garfield Sobers Trophy for ICC Cricketer of the Decade (2011–2020).
* Padma Shri and Rajiv Gandhi Khel Ratna awardee, India's prestigious civilian and sports honors.

## Playing Style

Kohli is fundamentally a textbook batsman who relies on superb technique, flawless footwork, and intense concentration rather than brute power. Primarily a wristy accumulator, he excels in rotating the strike effortlessly and pacing run chases with mathematical precision. His trademark shots include the elegant cover drive and a flick off his pads. As he evolved, his game adapted to feature aggressive intent from ball one in white-ball cricket, backed by a fierce competitive spirit and mental toughness.

## Interesting Facts

* Kohli was the captain who led India to their historic maiden Test series victory on Australian soil during the 2018–19 tour.
* He is the only player to have played for just a single franchise, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, across every season of the Indian Premier League since its inception in 2008.
* He adopted a strict vegan diet starting around 2018, which he credits for improving his physical fitness and recovery times on the field.
