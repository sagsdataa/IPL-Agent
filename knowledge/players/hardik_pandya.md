## Hardik Pandya

## Basic information

Full Name: Hardik Himanshu Pandya
Country: India
Role: All-rounder
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm fast-medium
IPL Teams: Mumbai Indians, Gujarat Titans

## Career Summary

Hardik Pandya made his international debut for India in January 2016 in a Twenty20 International against Australia. Known for his explosive lower-order batting and impactful medium-pace bowling, he quickly established himself as a key figure in India's limited-overs squads. He played a pivotal role in India's white-ball setups, participating in multiple ICC T20 World Cups and ICC Cricket World Cups, eventually taking on leadership responsibilities. In the Indian Premier League, he captained the Gujarat Titans to a title victory in their debut season in 2022 before returning to lead the Mumbai Indians.

## Major achievements

- Won the Indian Premier League (IPL) title as captain of the Gujarat Titans in 2022.
- Named Player of the Match in multiple crucial T20I and ODI fixtures for India.
- Reached the milestone of scoring 1,000 runs and taking 50 wickets in T20 Internationals.
- Led India in T20I series victories as captain.

## Playing Style

Hardik Pandya is a dynamic and aggressive right-handed batsman who excels in finishing innings with a high strike rate, particularly known for his powerful hitting down the ground and over the leg side. As a bowler, he operates as a seam-bowling all-rounder capable of hitting hard lengths and picking up crucial breakthrough wickets in the middle overs. He is also an athletic fielder with a strong throwing arm.

## Interesting Facts

- Hardik and his older brother Krunal Pandya both played together for the Mumbai Indians and the Indian national cricket team.
- He started his cricketing journey under the guidance of Kiran More in Vadodara after his family moved from Surat.
- He was the first captain to win the IPL trophy in a team's debut season since Rajasthan Royals in 2008.
