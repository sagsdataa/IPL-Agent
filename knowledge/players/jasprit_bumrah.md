## Jasprit Bumrah

## Basic information

Full Name: Jasprit Jasbirsingh Bumrah
Country: India
Role: Bowler
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm fast
IPL Teams: Mumbai Indians

## Career Summary

Jasprit Bumrah is widely regarded as one of the premier fast bowlers in modern international cricket. Making his international debut for India in January 2016 during a limited-overs tour of Australia, he quickly established himself as a lethal weapon due to his unorthodox bowling action and extreme accuracy. He made his Test debut in South Africa in January 2018. Over the years, Bumrah became the spearhead of India's bowling attack across all formats—Test matches, One Day Internationals, and Twenty20 Internationals. He has played a crucial role in numerous historic victories for India, including overseas Test series wins and consistent performances in ICC tournaments. In the Indian Premier League, he has been a stalwart for the Mumbai Indians, contributing heavily to multiple championship titles with his clutch bowling performances in powerplays and death overs.

## Major achievements

*   Fastest Indian fast bowler to reach 50, 100, and 150 wickets in Test matches.
*   Hat-trick in Test cricket against the West Indies in 2019.
*   ICC Men's T20 World Cup champion in 2024, winning the Player of the Tournament award.
*   Multiple-time Indian Premier League champion with the Mumbai Indians.
*   Ranked as the number one bowler in the ICC Test bowling rankings.

## Playing Style

Bumrah is famous for his unique, short run-up and hyper-extended bowling arm, which generates deceptive pace and steep bounce. He possesses a lethal yorker, utilized heavily in the death overs of limited-overs matches to stifle run-scoring and pick up crucial wickets. Beyond his yorkers, he excels with effective slower deliveries, sharp bouncers, and the ability to seam the ball both ways with a traditional upright seam position. His control over line and length makes him exceptionally economical under pressure.

## Interesting Facts

*   Bumrah was scouted by the Mumbai Indians coaching staff during a domestic T20 match in 2013.
*   He holds the world record for scoring the most runs in a single over of Test cricket, hitting Stuart Broad for 35 runs in 2022.
*   He captained the Indian Test team during the rescheduled fifth Test against England in 2022.
