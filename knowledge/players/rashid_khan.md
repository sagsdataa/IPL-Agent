## Rashid Khan

## Basic information

Full Name: Rashid Khan Arman
Country: Afghanistan
Role: Bowler (Leg Spinner)
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Leg-break googly
IPL Teams: Sunrisers Hyderabad, Gujarat Titans

## Career Summary

Rashid Khan made his international debut for Afghanistan in October 2015 in an ODI against Zimbabwe at the age of 17. He quickly rose through the ranks to become one of the premier spin bowlers in modern limited-overs cricket. Known for his rapid pace for a leg-spinner and deceptive variations, particularly the googly, he became the fastest bowler to take 100 wickets in One Day Internationals. He has played franchise Twenty20 cricket extensively around the globe, establishing himself as a marquee draft pick in leagues such as the Indian Premier League, Big Bash League, and Caribbean Premier League. He also served as the captain of the Afghanistan T20 team.

## Major achievements

Rashid holds the record for being the fastest bowler to reach 100 ODI wickets, achieving the milestone in 44 matches. He is also the fastest to 50 T20 International wickets. He took a hat-trick for Afghanistan against Ireland in 2019. In the Indian Premier League, he won the championship title with the Gujarat Titans in 2022, playing a crucial role as vice-captain and lead spinner. He has been named the ICC Men's T20 Player of the Decade for the period 2011–2020. 

## Playing Style

Rashid bowls leg-spin with a very fast bowling action and minimal run-up, relying on his quick arm speed to deceive batters through the air rather than relying on massive turn off the pitch. His delivery speed often exceeds 90 kilometers per hour, making it difficult for batters to adjust their length. His primary weapon is his disguised googly, which spins the opposite way and looks identical to his stock delivery. As a lower-order batter, he is known for his aggressive style, capable of hitting crucial boundaries in the death overs.

## Interesting Facts

Rashid Khan is the tenth of twelve siblings and grew up watching highlights of Shahid Afridi, whom he idolized. He was named an honorary citizen of Adelaide during his stint with the Adelaide Strikers in the Big Bash League. He is actively involved in charitable work through the Rashid Khan Foundation, which supports healthcare and education in Afghanistan.
