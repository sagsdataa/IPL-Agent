## Ravindra Jadeja

## Basic information

Full Name: Ravindra Jadeja
Country: India
Role: All-rounder
Batting Style: Left-hand bat
Bowling Style: Slow left-arm orthodox
IPL Teams: Rajasthan Royals, Kochi Tuskers Kerala, Chennai Super Kings, Gujarat Lions

## Career Summary

Ravindra Jadeja made his international debut in an ODI against Sri Lanka in February 2009. Known for his tenacious competitive spirit, he quickly established himself as a vital cog in the Indian team across all formats. In Test cricket, he became the fastest Indian left-arm bowler to reach 100, 200, and 300 wickets. In limited-overs cricket, his sharp fielding, economical bowling, and crucial lower-order batting have contributed significantly to India's success in major ICC tournaments.

## Major achievements

- Reached the milestone of 300 Test wickets for India.
- Scored multiple triple centuries in first-class cricket (Ranji Trophy).
- Ranked as the ICC No. 1 Test all-rounder.
- Won multiple IPL titles with Chennai Super Kings.
- Part of the Indian team that won the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.

## Playing Style

Jadeja is a dynamic spin-bowling all-rounder. With the ball, he relies on relentless accuracy, subtle variations in trajectory and pace, and extracting sharp turn from the pitch. As a batsman, he is aggressive and capable of building crucial partnerships or accelerating the scoring rate in the death overs. Internationally renowned as one of the greatest modern fielders, he contributes immensely with direct hits, athletic catches, and saving vital runs.

## Interesting Facts

- Nicknamed "Sir Jadeja" following a series of humorous internet memes praising his all-round abilities.
- He comes from a humble background; his father worked as a watchman.
- He is an expert horse rider and often shares videos of his horses at his farmhouse in Gujarat.
- He holds the rare record of scoring three triple centuries in first-class domestic cricket.
