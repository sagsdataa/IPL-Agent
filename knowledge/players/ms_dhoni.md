## MS Dhoni

## Basic information

Full Name: Mahendra Singh Dhoni
Country: India

Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm medium
IPL Teams: Chennai Super Kings, Rising Pune Supergiant

## Career Summary

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is a legendary Indian cricketer who played international cricket from 2004 to 2019. He is widely regarded as one of the greatest captains, finishers, and wicketkeepers in the history of the sport. Under his leadership, India reached the number one position in the ICC Test rankings in 2009. He announced his retirement from international cricket in August 2020, leaving behind a legacy of immense tactical brilliance and calm composure under pressure.

## Major achievements

Dhoni is the only captain in international cricket history to win all three major ICC trophies: the ICC T20 World Cup in 2007, the ICC Cricket World Cup in 2011, and the ICC Champions Trophy in 2013. He led India to victory in the 2011 World Cup final with a historic unbeaten 91 runs. In the Indian Premier League, he has captained Chennai Super Kings to multiple championship titles. He holds the record for the most international matches as a captain.

## Playing Style

As a batter, Dhoni was known for his aggressive lower-order power-hitting, anchored by a unique technique that included his trademark "helicopter shot." As a wicketkeeper, he was famous for lightning-fast stumpings and unorthodox, efficient glovework. His ultimate hallmark as a player was his exceptional calmness in tense run-chases, earning him the moniker "Captain Cool."

## Interesting Facts

Before establishing himself as a professional cricketer, Dhoni worked as a Travelling Ticket Examiner (TTE) for the Indian Railways at the Kharagpur railway station. He holds an honorary lieutenant colonel rank in the Territorial Army of India. A biopic titled "MS Dhoni: The Untold Story" was released in 2016, chronicling his journey from a ticket collector to a World Cup-winning captain.
