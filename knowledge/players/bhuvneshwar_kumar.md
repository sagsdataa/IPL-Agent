## Bhuvneshwar Kumar

Full Name: Bhuvneshwar Kumar Singh
Country: India
Role: Bowler
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm medium
IPL Teams: Royal Challengers Bangalore, Pune Warriors India, Sunrisers Hyderabad

## Career Summary
Bhuvneshwar Kumar made his international debut for India in a T20I against Pakistan in December 2012. Known for his exceptional swing bowling and calmness under pressure, he quickly established himself as a vital part of India's pace attack across all formats. He became the first bowler to dismiss Sachin Tendulkar for a duck in domestic first-class cricket. He has represented India in numerous ICC tournaments, playing a crucial role in India's victory at the 2013 ICC Champions Trophy.

## Major achievements
*   Winner of the Indian Premier League (IPL) Purple Cap for most wickets in consecutive seasons (2016, 2017).
*   First Indian bowler to take five-wicket hauls in all three formats of the game (Test, ODI, T20I).
*   Played a pivotal role in Sunrisers Hyderabad winning their maiden IPL title in 2016.
*   Awarded BCCI's Polly Umrigar Award for International Cricketer of the Year.

## Playing Style
Bhuvneshwar Kumar is a classic swing bowler rather than an express-pace merchant. He relies heavily on the movement of the ball in the air and off the pitch, making him particularly lethal during the Powerplay overs in limited-overs cricket. He possesses the ability to swing the ball both ways. In the death overs, he utilizes precise yorkers and clever slower deliveries to restrict run-scoring. He is also a capable lower-order batsman who has played several rescue knocks for his team.

## Interesting Facts
*   His sister, Mangla Chaudhary, was the one who encouraged him to take up cricket seriously and bought him his first proper cricket kit.
*   He is nicknamed "Bhuvi" by fans and teammates.
*   He is married to Nupur Nagar, and they have a daughter.
