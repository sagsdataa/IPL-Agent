## Rohit Sharma

## Basic information

Full Name: Rohit Gurunath Sharma
Country: India
Role: Opening Batsman
Batting Style: Right-hand bat
Bowling Style: Right-arm off break
IPL Teams: Deccan Chargers, Mumbai Indians

## Career Summary

Rohit Sharma is one of the most successful opening batsmen in modern international cricket, known for his elegant strokeplay and exceptional ability to score heavily in limited-overs formats. Making his international debut in 2007, he initially played in the middle order before transitioning to open the batting in 2013, a move that transformed his career. He has captained the Indian national team across all formats and led the Mumbai Indians to multiple Indian Premier League titles.

## Major achievements

Rohit holds the world record for the highest individual score in One Day International cricket, with 264 runs against Sri Lanka in 2014. He is the only player in cricket history to score three double centuries in One Day Internationals. He also holds the record for the most centuries in men's Twenty20 Internationals and the most centuries in the history of the ICC Men's Cricket World Cup, sharing the overall record for most World Cup hundreds. As captain, he led India to victory in the 2024 ICC Men's T20 World Cup.

## Playing Style

Known for his remarkable timing and effortless power, Rohit is particularly famous for his exceptional pull shot against fast bowling and his ability to hit big sixes with minimal apparent effort. He is renowned for pacing his innings meticulously, starting cautiously before accelerating drastically to convert standard hundreds into massive, game-defining scores. 

## Interesting Facts

Rohit was named after the famous Indian film actor and director, Raj Kapoor, whose birth name was Raj Kapoor. He is a passionate animal welfare advocate and serves as an official WWF-India rhinoceros ambassador, actively supporting campaigns to save the endangered greater one-horned rhinoceros.
