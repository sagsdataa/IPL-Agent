# Orange Cap

## Definition
The Orange Cap is awarded annually to the leading run-scorer in the Indian Premier League (IPL) season. 

## Purpose
This award was introduced to recognize individual batting excellence, celebrate top-tier run-scoring performances, and add excitement and competition among batters throughout the tournament.

## Key Rules
* The player with the most runs in the current IPL season wears the Orange Cap during matches.
* If two or more batters score the same number of runs, the player with the superior strike rate is awarded the cap.
* The leading run-scorer in the final match retains the Orange Cap permanently for that season.
* The holder of the cap wears a distinct orange-colored helmet while batting on the field to visually identify them as the tournament's top scorer.

## Example
During the 2023 IPL season, Royal Challengers Bengaluru batsman Faf du Plessis held the Orange Cap for a major portion of the tournament, but Gujarat Titans opener Shubman Gill ultimately claimed the final award by scoring 890 runs, the second-highest total in a single IPL season.

## Recent Changes
While the core criteria of awarding the player with the most runs remains unchanged, the design and fabric of the cap have evolved alongside the official kit sponsor updates to match modern sportswear standards.
