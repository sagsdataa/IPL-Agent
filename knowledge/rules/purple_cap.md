# Purple Cap

## Definition
The Purple Cap is an official Indian Premier League accolade awarded annually to the leading wicket-taker of the tournament.

## Purpose
This rule was introduced to incentivize and recognize exceptional bowling performances, ensuring bowlers receive equal prominence alongside batters throughout the season.

## Key Rules
- The bowler with the most wickets during the season wears the Purple Cap while fielding.
- If two or more bowlers tie on total wickets, the player with the superior economy rate claims the cap.
- If the tie persists including the economy rate, the bowler with the better bowling average is awarded the cap.
- The player holding the cap at the end of the final match officially wins the Purple Cap award.
- During the match, the current leading wicket-taker fields with the cap, and if a fielder overtakes him during the game, he takes over wearing it in the next innings or match depending on official handover.

## Example
During the 2013 Indian Premier League season, Dwayne Bravo of the Chennai Super Kings took 32 wickets, which set a then-record and secured him the Purple Cap for that tournament.

## Recent Changes
While the core criteria of most wickets followed by superior economy rate remain unchanged, the cap's physical design has evolved for modern broadcast aesthetics, and the award ceremony is now a staple of the official post-final presentation.
