# Impact Player Rule

## Definition
The Impact Player rule is a tactical substitution regulation in the Indian Premier League (IPL) that allows teams to replace one playing member during a match with a substitute player from a list of five nominated substitutes.

## Purpose
This rule was introduced to make IPL matches more dynamic, enhance strategic depth, encourage all-rounder performances, and provide teams with the ability to counter match situations mid-game.

## Key Rules
* Each team must nominate five substitute players alongside their starting playing eleven before the toss.
* Only one of these substitutes can be used as an Impact Player per match.
* The substitution can be made before the start of the innings, at the end of an over, at the fall of a wicket, or when a batter retires.
* If a team fields four overseas players in the starting eleven, the Impact Player must be an Indian player. If fewer than four overseas players are in the starting eleven, an overseas player can be used as the Impact Player.
* The replaced player takes no further part in the match, neither batting nor fielding.

## Example
Chennai Super Kings bats first and loses early wickets. During the innings break, they substitute a fast bowler with an extra batter to strengthen their batting lineup. 

## Recent Changes
Since its introduction in the 2023 season, the rule was refined ahead of the 2024 season to allow teams to use the Impact Player seamlessly without mandatory over-restriction declarations at the toss, though it has continued to spark debates among cricketers regarding its impact on traditional all-rounders.
