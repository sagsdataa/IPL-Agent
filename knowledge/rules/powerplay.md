# PowerPlay

## Definition
The PowerPlay is a set of fielding restrictions in the Indian Premier League (IPL) that limits the number of fielders permitted outside the 30-yard fielding circle during specific overs of an innings.

## Purpose
This rule was introduced to encourage attacking batting, maximize boundary scoring in the early and middle stages of an innings, and add strategic depth to limited-overs cricket.

## Key Rules
- The innings is divided into three distinct PowerPlay phases: the mandatory PowerPlay and two tactical strategic timeouts.
- Overs 1 to 6 represent the mandatory PowerPlay, during which a maximum of two fielders are allowed outside the 30-yard circle.
- From overs 7 to 20, a maximum of five fielders are permitted outside the 30-yard circle.
- If the fielding team fails to complete their overs within the stipulated time, a penalty is imposed reducing the number of outfielders allowed outside the circle.

## Example
During the first over of an IPL match, the batting team hits three consecutive boundaries because the fielding captain can only place two fielders in the deep, leaving vast gaps in the outfield.

## Recent Changes
In recent IPL seasons, the strategic timeout was split into two intervals—one during the first ten overs and the second during the final ten overs—allowing teams to re-evaluate their PowerPlay tactics.
