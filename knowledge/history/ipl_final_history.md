# IPL Final History


## Overview
The Indian Premier League (IPL) final is the pinnacle match of the annual Twenty20 cricket tournament, determining the champion of the season. Since its inception in 2008, the final has grown into one of the most-watched sporting events globally, captivating millions of fans with dramatic finishes and high-stakes cricket.


## Timeline
The inaugural IPL final took place on June 1, 2008, at the DY Patil Stadium in Navi Mumbai, where the Rajasthan Royals defeated the Chennai Super Kings. Over the years, the venue for the summit clash has rotated across various international cricket stadiums in India, occasionally moving overseas due to general elections or safety concerns, such as in 2009 (South Africa) and 2014 (UAE).


## Significant Events
Several finals stand out in IPL history. In 2008, rookie captain Shane Warne guided the underdog Rajasthan Royals to a thrilling last-ball victory over Chennai. In 2017, Mumbai Indians defended a modest total of 129 against Rising Pune Supergiant, winning by a single run. The 2019 final saw Mumbai defeat Chennai by the exact same margin of one run, courtesy of a final-ball wicket by Lasith Malinga.


## Records
Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings share the record for the most successful franchises, each claiming five IPL titles. Rohit Sharma holds the record for the most IPL titles won by an individual player, with six (five as captain of Mumbai Indians, one as a player for Deccan Chargers). In the summit clashes, Anil Kumble holds the best bowling figures in a final, taking 4 for 16 for Royal Challengers Bangalore in 2009, albeit in a losing cause. Shane Watson is the only player to score a century in an IPL final, achieving the feat for Chennai Super Kings in 2018.


## Interesting Facts
No team batting first defended a total in the IPL final for the first six seasons until the trend began to shift. The Chennai Super Kings hold the record for the most appearances in an IPL final, reaching the title clash ten times. Additionally, the final match is traditionally preceded by a grand closing ceremony featuring performances by major global and Bollywood music artists.
