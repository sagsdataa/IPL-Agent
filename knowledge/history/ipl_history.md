# Indian Premier League


## Overview
The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India, founded by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) in 2007. It features franchise teams representing Indian cities and states. The tournament typically takes place annually during the summer months and has grown to become the most-watched cricket league globally, revolutionizing the sport through a blend of entertainment and international player participation.


## Timeline
- **2007:** The BCCI launched the IPL following India's victory in the inaugural ICC World Twenty20.
- **2008:** The inaugural season took place, won by the Rajasthan Royals.
- **2010:** The league introduced two new franchises, Kochi Tuskers Kerala and Pune Warriors India, though both later exited.
- **2016:** Two-time champions Chennai Super Kings and Rajasthan Royals were suspended for two years due to a betting scandal.
- **2022:** The league expanded to a ten-team format with the addition of the Gujarat Titans and Lucknow Super Giants.


## Significant Events
The 2008 inaugural final saw the underdog Rajasthan Royals defeat the Chennai Super Kings in a thrilling last-ball finish. In 2010, the IPL became the first sporting event in the world to be broadcast live on YouTube. The 2016 season marked a historic individual batting performance when Virat Kohli scored 973 runs, the most in a single season. The 2022 season final drew a Guinness World Record crowd of over 100,000 spectators at the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad.


## Records
Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings share the record for the most tournament victories, with five titles each. Virat Kohli holds the record for the most career runs scored in the tournament's history. Yuzvendra Chahal holds the record for taking the most career wickets. The highest team total in an innings is 287 for 3, scored by Sunrisers Hyderabad against Royal Challengers Bengaluru in 2024. Chris Gayle holds the record for the highest individual score in a single innings, with 175 not out achieved in 2013.


## Interesting Facts
The IPL operates on a unique salary cap system using an auction format where franchises bid for players. It was the first cricket league to introduce strategic timeouts during matches. The purple cap is awarded annually to the leading wicket-taker, while the orange cap goes to the leading run-scorer.
