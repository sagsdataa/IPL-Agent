# Greatest IPL Records


## Overview
The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a premier Twenty20 cricket league established by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) in 2007. It features franchise teams representing different Indian cities and attracts the world's best cricketers, making it the most-watched cricket league globally.


## Timeline
- **2007:** The BCCI announced the creation of the Indian Premier League.
- **2008:** The inaugural IPL season commenced, with the Rajasthan Royals winning the first title.
- **2010:** Chennai Super Kings secured their first of multiple championship titles.
- **2013:** Mumbai Indians won their maiden IPL trophy, beginning a dominant era.
- **2022:** Two new franchises, Gujarat Titans and Lucknow Super Giants, joined the league, expanding it to ten teams.


## Significant Events
- **The Inaugural Match (2008):** Brendon McCullum scored a historic 158 not out for Kolkata Knight Riders, setting the stage for the league's explosive popularity.
- **The 2008 Final:** Rajasthan Royals defeated Chennai Super Kings off the final ball in a thrilling climax to the first season.
- **The 2022 Mega Final:** Gujarat Titans won the championship in their debut season in front of a record home crowd at Ahmedabad.


## Records
- **Highest Team Total:** Sunrisers Hyderabad scored 287/3 against Royal Challengers Bengaluru in 2024.
- **Most Title Wins:** Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings share the record with five titles each.
- **All-Time Leading Run Scorer:** Virat Kohli holds the record for the most runs in IPL history, surpassing 8,000 runs.
- **All-Time Leading Wicket Taker:** Yuzvendra Chahal is the highest wicket-taker in the history of the tournament.
- **Highest Individual Score:** Chris Gayle scored 175 not out off 66 balls for Royal Challengers Bengaluru against Pune Warriors India in 2013.


## Interesting Facts
- The IPL was the first sporting event in the world to be broadcasted live on YouTube in 2010.
- Chris Gayle holds the record for the fastest century in IPL history, reaching the milestone in just 30 balls during his 175-run knock.
- Mumbai Indians is the only team to win the Champions League Twenty20 twice alongside their five IPL titles.
