# Evolution Of IPL

## Overview
The Indian Premier League (IPL) is a professional Twenty20 cricket league in India, founded by the Board of Control for Cricket in India (BCCI) in 2007. It revolutionized global cricket by blending sports and entertainment, becoming the world's most lucrative and attended cricket league.


## Timeline
- **2007:** BCCI announced the establishment of the IPL following India's T20 World Cup victory.
- **2008:** The inaugural season took place, featuring eight franchises and culminating in a Rajasthan Royals victory.
- **2016:** Expansion teams Rising Pune Supergiant and Gujarat Lions played during suspensions of Chennai Super Kings and Rajasthan Royals.
- **2022:** The league expanded to ten teams with the addition of Gujarat Titans and Lucknow Super Giants.


## Significant Events
- **2008 Launch:** Lalit Modi spearheaded the launch, introducing city-based franchise auctions, player drafts, and cheerleaders.
- **2009 South Africa Move:** General elections forced the tournament to relocate to South Africa at short notice, where the Deccan Chargers won.
- **2020 UAE Bubble:** The COVID-19 pandemic necessitated moving the tournament to the United Arab Emirates, successfully conducted inside strict bio-secure bubbles.


## Records
- **Most Titles:** Mumbai Indians and Chennai Super Kings share the record with five titles each.
- **Highest Team Total:** Sunrisers Hyderabad scored 287/3 against Royal Challengers Bengaluru in 2024.
- **Most Runs:** Virat Kohli holds the record for the most career runs in IPL history.
- **Most Wickets:** Yuzvendra Chahal is the leading wicket-taker in the tournament.


## Interesting Facts
- The IPL was the first sporting event in the world to be broadcast live on YouTube in 2010.
- Rajasthan Royals won the first-ever season under the captaincy of Shane Warne with a modest budget compared to other star-studded franchises.
- The 2023 final between Chennai Super Kings and Gujarat Titans was watched by a record peak concurrent livestream viewership of over 32 million on digital platforms.
