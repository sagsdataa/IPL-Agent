# Punjab Kings

## Basic information

Home Ground: M. A. Chidambaram Stadium (Historically PCA Stadium, Mohali and Maharaja Yadavindra Singh International Cricket Stadium, Mullanpur)
Founded: 2008
Current Captain: Sam Curran (2024 season context)
Head Coach: Ricky Ponting

## Team History
Founded in 2008 as the Mohali-based franchise, the team was originally named Kings XI Punjab before rebranding as Punjab Kings in 2021. Co-owned by a consortium including Preity Zinta, Ness Wadia, Mohit Burman, and Karan Paul, the franchise has historically experienced mixed fortunes in the Indian Premier League, frequently undergoing leadership and squad overhauls.

## Major achievements
The franchise's most notable achievement is finishing as runners-up in the 2014 IPL season, where they were defeated by the Kolkata Knight Riders in a high-scoring final. They also reached the semi-finals in the inaugural 2008 season. 

## Notable Players
Key players who have represented the franchise include Adam Gilchrist, Shaun Marsh, Glenn Maxwell, KL Rahul, Chris Gayle, Virender Sehwag, Shikhar Dhawan, and Arshdeep Singh. Shaun Marsh was the inaugural Orange Cap winner in 2008.

## Playing Identity
Punjab Kings are widely recognized for an aggressive, high-risk, high-reward batting approach. They often boast explosive overseas hitters and dynamic Indian talent, though consistency has historically been a challenge for the squad.

## Interesting Facts
- Shaun Marsh scored the most runs in the inaugural 2008 IPL season, earning the first-ever Orange Cap.
- Kings XI Punjab recorded the highest successful run chase in IPL history (at the time) by chasing down 262 against Kolkata Knight Riders in 2024.
- The franchise has played its home matches across multiple venues, including Dharamshala, Indore, Mohali, and Mullanpur.
