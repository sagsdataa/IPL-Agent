# Lucknow Super Giants

## Basic information

Home Ground: BRSABV Ekana Cricket Stadium, Lucknow  
Founded: 2021  
Current Captain: Rishabh Pant *(Note: Captaincy subject to annual auction/retention dynamics)*  
Head Coach: Justin Langer  

## Team History
Lucknow Super Giants (LSG) entered the Indian Premier League as one of the two new franchises in the 2022 season, owned by the RPSG Group. Led initially by KL Rahul, the team made an immediate impact by qualifying for the playoffs in their debut season and repeating the feat in 2023. LSG quickly established itself as a competitive force in the tournament, known for strategic squad building and balanced performances.

## Major achievements
- Qualified for the IPL Playoffs in their debut season (2022).
- Reached the IPL Playoffs for a second consecutive time in 2023.

## Notable Players
- KL Rahul
- Quinton de Kock
- Marcus Stoinis
- Nicholas Pooran

- Mohsin Khan

## Playing Identity
LSG is characterized by a balanced and data-driven approach to T20 cricket. The team relies on a solid top-order foundation, aggressive middle-over hitting, and a versatile bowling attack capable of defending totals on various pitches, particularly utilizing their home conditions effectively.

## Interesting Facts
- The franchise was purchased by the RPSG Group for a whopping INR 7,090 crore (approx. $940 million), making it one of the most expensive teams in IPL history at the time of its inception.
- A nationwide contest was held to choose the team name, ultimately selecting "Super Giants" to reflect strength and scale.
- Legendary Indian cricketer Gautam Gambhir served as the team's first mentor, guiding them to successive playoff appearances in their first two years.
- Their home venue, the BRSABV Ekana Cricket Stadium, features one of the largest playing fields in India, influencing the team's tactical approach to spin bowling and boundary defense.
