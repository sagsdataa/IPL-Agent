# Mumbai Indians

## Basic information

Home Ground: Wankhede Stadium
Founded: 2007
Current Captain: Hardik Pandya
Head Coach: Mahela Jayawardene

## Team History
Mumbai Indians (MI) is a franchise cricket team based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, competing in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Owned by Reliance Industries through its subsidiary Indiawin Sports, the team was established in 2007. Over the years, Mumbai Indians have grown to become one of the most successful, financially powerful, and widely supported franchises in the history of the tournament.

## Major achievements
Mumbai Indians are joint-record five-time IPL champions, lifting the trophy in 2013, 2015, 2017, 2019, and 2020. They also won the Champions League Twenty20 twice in 2011 and 2013. The franchise is known for its dominance in high-pressure matches, having won all five of their IPL final appearances under the leadership of Rohit Sharma.

## Notable Players
Sachin Tendulkar, Rohit Sharma, Jasprit Bumrah, Kieron Pollard, Lasith Malinga, Suryakumar Yadav, Hardik Pandya, and Ambati Rayudu.

## Playing Identity
Mumbai Indians are renowned for their aggressive brand of cricket, exceptional talent scouting, and a strong emphasis on building a deep, balanced squad. They possess a reputation as slow starters in the tournament group stages who traditionally peak during the second half of the season, often referred to as "slow starters" before dominating the playoffs.

## Interesting Facts
* Mumbai Indians was the first IPL team to win the tournament five times.
* Sachin Tendulkar was the team's icon player and captain in the early seasons before transitioning into a mentor role.
* Wankhede Stadium serves as their iconic home ground, known for its electric atmosphere and red-soil pitch.
* MI introduced the world to numerous uncapped Indian talents who later became international stars, including Jasprit Bumrah and Hardik Pandya.
