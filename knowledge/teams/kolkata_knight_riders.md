# Kolkata Knight Riders

## Basic information

Home Ground: Eden Gardens, Kolkata
Founded: 2008
Current Captain: Shreyas Iyer
Head Coach: Chandrakant Pandit

## Team History
Kolkata Knight Riders (KKR) is a franchise cricket team representing Kolkata in the Indian Premier League, owned by Bollywood icon Shah Rukh Khan, actress Juhi Chawla, and Jay Mehta. After enduring mixed results and rebuilding phases in the early years of the tournament, the franchise established itself as a powerhouse under the leadership of Gautam Gambhir in the 2010s. KKR is recognized as one of the most commercially successful and followed teams in the league.

## Major achievements
Kolkata Knight Riders have won the IPL championship three times, securing titles in 2012, 2014, and 2024. They also hold the record for the longest winning streak by an IPL team, winning 14 consecutive matches across the 2014 and 2015 seasons.

## Notable Players
Gautam Gambhir, Sunil Narine, Andre Russell, Dinesh Karthik, Jacques Kallis, Yusuf Pathan, Robin Uthappa, and Mitchell Starc.

## Playing Identity
KKR is traditionally known for a fearless and aggressive brand of cricket, heavily reliant on world-class spin-bowling all-rounders and explosive lower-order batting. The team motto, "Korbo, Lorbo, Jeetbo Re" (We will do it, fight for it, and win it), defines their competitive spirit on the field.

## Interesting Facts
- KKR was the first IPL franchise to appoint different captains for different formats and the first to introduce a fan anthem.
- Their home venue, Eden Gardens, is one of the largest cricket stadiums in the world and is renowned for generating one of the most intimidating atmospheres for visiting teams.
- Sunil Narine is one of the most impactful overseas players in IPL history, having won the Most Valuable Player award multiple times while representing KKR.
- The team colors transitioned from black and gold to purple and gold ahead of the 2010 season.
