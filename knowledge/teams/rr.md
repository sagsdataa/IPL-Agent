# Rajasthan Royals

## Basic information

Home Ground: Sawai Mansingh Stadium, Jaipur
Founded: 2008
Current Captain: Sanju Samson
Head Coach: Rahul Dravid

## Team History
Rajasthan Royals was established in 2008 as one of the founding franchises of the Indian Premier League. Representing the states of Rajasthan and Gujarat, the franchise became famous for building a team without star-studded names in its inaugural season. Under the inspirational leadership of Shane Warne, the underdogs defied all odds to lift the championship trophy in the very first edition of the tournament. Over the years, the franchise has experienced various ownership transitions and a two-year suspension, but it has remained a competitive force and reached the final again in 2022.

## Major achievements
- Indian Premier League Champions: 2008
- Indian Premier League Runners-up: 2022
- Champions League Twenty20 Semi-finalists: 2008, 2013

## Playing Identity
Rajasthan Royals are known for unearthing raw domestic talent and providing a platform for young, uncapped players to showcase their skills on the global stage. The team often adopts an aggressive, fearless batting approach coupled with smart, adaptive bowling tactics, particularly in defending totals or setting up chases through dynamic opening partnerships.

## Interesting Facts
- Rajasthan Royals won the inaugural IPL season in 2008 under the dual role of captain and coach played by the legendary leg-spinner Shane Warne.
- The franchise holds the record for the highest successful run chase in IPL history, successfully hunting down 224 runs against the Punjab Kings in 2020.
- Rajasthan Royals pioneered the "Super Over" rule utilization in the IPL during a dramatic group stage match against Kolkata Knight Riders in 2009.
- The team jersey features a prominent pink color scheme, symbolizing support for women's empowerment in Rajasthan through their 'Pink Promise' initiative.
