# Chennai Super Kings

## Basic information

Home Ground: MA Chidambaram Stadium (Chepauk)
Founded: 2008
Current Captain: Ruturaj Gaikwad
Head Coach: Stephen Fleming

## Team History
Chennai Super Kings (CSK) is one of the most successful and consistent franchises in the Indian Premier League (IPL). Owned by India Cements through Chennai Super Kings Cricket Limited, the franchise was established in 2008. CSK was suspended from the league for two seasons (2016 and 2017) due to the involvement of officials in the 2013 betting scandal, but made a triumphant return by winning the title in 2018.

## Major achievements
CSK has won five IPL titles (2010, 2011, 2018, 2021, and 2023), sharing the record for the most tournament victories. They hold the record for the most appearances in the IPL playoffs and finals, having reached the final ten times and the playoffs twelve times in their active seasons. They also won the Champions League Twenty20 twice in 2010 and 2014.

## Notable Players
MS Dhoni, Suresh Raina, Ravindra Jadeja, Dwayne Bravo, Ravichandran Ashwin, Faf du Plessis, Michael Hussey, Brendon McCullum, Ambati Rayudu, and Ruturaj Gaikwad.

## Playing Identity
CSK is widely recognized for its calm leadership, tactical brilliance, and backing of experienced players, earning them the moniker "Dad's Army." Under the captaincy of MS Dhoni and coaching of Stephen Fleming, the team emphasizes consistency, process-oriented approaches, and retaining a core group of players over auction cycles. 

## Interesting Facts
* CSK is the first-ever unicorn sports enterprise in India, valued significantly in the corporate market.
* MS Dhoni has captained the franchise in every season he has played for them.
* CSK has never failed to qualify for the playoffs under MS Dhoni's captaincy in the seasons they were not suspended.
* The team has a fiercely loyal fan base known as the "Yellow Army," transforming away grounds into home matches with massive yellow crowds.
