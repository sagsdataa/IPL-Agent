# Delhi Capitals

## Basic information

Home Ground: Arun Jaitley Stadium, Delhi
Founded: 2008
Current Captain: Rishabh Pant
Head Coach: Hemang Badani

## Team History
Founded in 2008 as the Delhi Daredevils, the franchise was one of the original eight teams in the Indian Premier League. Owned jointly by the GMR Group and the JSW Group, the franchise was rebranded as the Delhi Capitals in December 2018. Despite early struggles and inconsistent performances, the team underwent a major resurgence starting in 2019, consistently qualifying for the playoffs under a refreshed leadership group.

## Major achievements
The Delhi Capitals reached their first-ever IPL final in the 2020 season, finishing as runners-up to the Mumbai Indians. The franchise also topped the league stage points table in 2021 and previously secured semi-final appearances in the inaugural 2008 and 2009 seasons.

## Notable Players
Rishabh Pant, David Warner, Shikhar Dhawan, Shreyas Iyer, Kagiso Rabada, Virender Sehwag, AB de Villiers, Gautam Gambhir, and Anrich Nortje.

## Playing Identity
Known for an aggressive, fearless approach with the bat and a lethal, pace-heavy bowling attack. The team places strong emphasis on nurturing young Indian talent alongside experienced international stars.

## Interesting Facts
* Virender Sehwag was the iconic player and early captain of the franchise, leading them to the semi-finals in 2008.
* The team played their home matches at the Shaheed Veer Narayan Singh International Cricket Stadium in Raipur as a secondary home ground between 2013 and 2015.
* Rishabh Pant holds the record for the most runs scored for the franchise in IPL history.
* The franchise changed its name from Delhi Daredevils to Delhi Capitals to signify a fresh start and represent the political capital of India.
