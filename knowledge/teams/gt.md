# Gujarat Titans

## Basic information

Home Ground: Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad
Founded: 2021
Current Captain: Shubman Gill
Head Coach: Ashish Nehra

## Team History

The Gujarat Titans were established in 2021 as one of the two new franchises entering the Indian Premier League for the 2022 season. Owned by the private equity firm CVC Capital Partners, the franchise quickly established itself as a powerhouse in the tournament. Based in Ahmedabad, the team plays its home matches at the world's largest cricket stadium. From their debut season, the Titans displayed exceptional resilience, tactical maturity, and consistency, transforming into one of the most successful expansion teams in league history under the leadership of Hardik Pandya and coach Ashish Nehra.

## Major achievements

The Gujarat Titans won the IPL championship in their inaugural season in 2022, defeating the Rajasthan Royals in the final. They followed this stellar debut with another dominant campaign in 2023, finishing as the runners-up after a thrilling final loss to the Chennai Super Kings. The team also secured the top position in the league standings in both 2022 and 2023.

## Notable Players

Hardik Pandya, Shubman Gill, Rashid Khan, Mohammed Shami, David Miller, Rahul Tewatia, and Mohit Sharma.

## Playing Identity

The Gujarat Titans are known for their calm demeanor under pressure, aggressive yet calculated batting approach, and a potent, disciplined bowling attack. The team prides itself on backing young and uncapped Indian talent alongside world-class international stars, fostering a strong culture of collective responsibility where different players step up as match-winners in crucial situations.

## Interesting Facts

- The Gujarat Titans won the IPL trophy in their very first appearance in the tournament in 2022.
- Their home venue, the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad, holds the record for the largest cricket stadium capacity in the world.
- Rashid Khan served as the team's vice-captain and played a pivotal role in their spin department from their debut season.
- Shubman Gill scored the highest number of runs in a single season for the franchise, winning the Orange Cap in 2023.
