# Sunrisers Hyderabad

## Basic information

Home Ground: Rajiv Gandhi International Cricket Stadium, Hyderabad
Founded: 2012
Current Captain: Pat Cummins
Head Coach: Daniel Vettori

## Team History
Sunrisers Hyderabad (SRH) was founded in 2012 after the termination of the Deccan Chargers franchise. Owned by Kalanithi Maran of the Sun TV Network, the team made its IPL debut in 2013 and immediately qualified for the playoffs. Over the years, SRH developed a reputation for having one of the most formidable bowling attacks in the tournament, anchoring their success around disciplined bowling units before transitioning into a high-scoring batting powerhouse.

## Major achievements
SRH won their maiden IPL title in 2016, defeating Royal Challengers Bangalore in the final under the captaincy of David Warner. They reached the IPL final again in 2018 and 2024, finishing as runners-up on both occasions. The franchise has qualified for the IPL playoffs multiple times.

## Notable Players
David Warner, Bhuvneshwar Kumar, Rashid Khan, Kane Williamson, Pat Cummins, Heinrich Klaasen, Travis Head, and Shikhar Dhawan.

## Playing Identity
Historically renowned for defending low totals through a world-class bowling attack, SRH underwent a massive tactical shift in recent seasons. They are now recognized for an ultra-aggressive, high-tempo batting style in the powerplay, aiming to set and chase massive totals.

## Interesting Facts
- David Warner is the leading run-scorer for the franchise and won the Orange Cap multiple times while playing for SRH.
- Bhuvneshwar Kumar holds the record for being the first Indian bowler to win the Purple Cap twice consecutively in 2016 and 2017.
- In the 2024 season, SRH broke the record for the highest team total in IPL history twice within a few weeks.
- The team anthem is "Orange Army," which is also the affectionate moniker used for the franchise's fanbase.
