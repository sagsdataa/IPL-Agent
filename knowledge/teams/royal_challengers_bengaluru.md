# Royal Challengers Bengaluru

## Basic information

Home Ground: M. Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru
Founded: 2008
Current Captain: Faf du Plessis
Head Coach: Andy Flower

## Team History
Royal Challengers Bengaluru, originally named Royal Challengers Bangalore, is owned by United Spirits and represents the city of Bengaluru in the Indian Premier League. The franchise has been one of the most popular and commercially significant teams in the tournament since its inception, boasting a massive and loyal fan base known for their passionate support under the rallying cry "Ee Sala Cup Namde."

## Major achievements
Despite never winning an IPL title, the franchise has reached the final on three occasions (2009, 2011, and 2016). They hold two of the highest team totals in IPL history (263/5 against Pune Warriors India in 2013 and 287/3 against Sunrisers Hyderabad in 2024). They also hold the record for the lowest team total in IPL history (49 all out against Kolkata Knight Riders in 2017).

## Notable Players
Virat Kohli, AB de Villiers, Chris Gayle, Anil Kumble, Glenn Maxwell, Faf du Plessis, and Yuzvendra Chahal.

## Playing Identity
The team is traditionally known for an aggressive, star-studded batting lineup that relies heavily on explosive stroke-play and entertainment value. Their home venue is famous for being a high-scoring ground due to its short boundaries and favorable batting conditions.

## Interesting Facts
- Virat Kohli is the only player in IPL history to represent a single franchise from the very first season through the present day.
- Chris Gayle scored the fastest century in cricket history (off 30 balls) playing for the franchise against Pune Warriors India in 2013, en route to his record-breaking score of 175 not out.
- The franchise retired the jersey numbers 17 (AB de Villiers) and 33 (Chris Gayle) to honor their immense contributions to the team.
- In 2024, the team achieved a historic turnaround by winning six consecutive matches to qualify for the playoffs after having only one win in their first eight games.
